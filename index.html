FAQ

Straight answers

Is $997 worth it for a 90-minute audit?

The audit isn't 90 minutes of talking — it's a written roadmap that identifies your three highest-ROI automation opportunities, ranked, with the math shown. Most owners find one item on that list that covers the fee many times over in the first year. $997 is a founding-client rate while we build local case studies; it goes up once those first 25 spots are filled. And the roadmap is yours: you can hand it to anyone to implement, including yourself. If you read it and don't think it was worth the fee, tell us and we'll refund it. You're never locked in.

How fast will I see results?

The audit and roadmap are delivered within a week of your session. If you move to Setup, your first automation is typically live within 2–4 weeks. We deliberately start with the fastest-payback opportunity, so the first thing we build is usually the one you feel immediately — fewer missed calls, faster follow-up, hours back in your week.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

It's the whole point. NVICTUS exists for owners who are great at their business and have zero interest in becoming software people. Everything we deploy is chosen because your team can actually run it. We do the setup with you, train your people in plain English, and you're never left with a system only we understand.

What makes NVICTUS different from an agency or a software vendor?

A vendor sells you their tool whether it fits or not. An agency sells you hours. We sell you an outcome: the right automation for your business, picked from the whole market, deployed fast, with the math justified up front. We're not married to any platform and we don't profit from complexity — the simpler the solution that gets you the result, the better we look.

What if I don't like what you build?

You see it working before you're asked to keep it. Setup is built in stages, and you sign off at each one — so there's no big reveal at the end that misses the mark. If the system doesn't do what we said it would, we fix it or you don't pay the balance. We'd rather lose a build than have you paying for something that sits unused.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. The audit is a one-off with nothing attached. Setup is a flat, per-project price you approve before we start. The retainer is month-to-month — cancel with 30 days' notice, and everything we've built stays yours and keeps running. We keep clients with results, not paperwork.
